# WayFinder Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Last Updated:** [Date]

This Privacy Policy describes how WayFinder ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the WayFinder mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Camera and Media Data:** We require camera access to capture video frames of your surroundings. These frames are temporarily sent to our backend servers for AI-based analysis to provide navigation guidance and threat detection.
- **Microphone and Voice Data:** We require microphone access to listen for the "WayFinder" wake word (processed entirely offline on your device) and to record voice questions when you explicitly ask the assistant. Voice recordings are converted to text and may be sent to our servers.
- **Authentication Data:** If you sign in via Google (Firebase Auth), we collect your email address and basic profile information to securely tie your interaction history to your account.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To provide real-time egocentric navigation assistance.
- To detect obstacles and hazards in your immediate path.
- To answer specific questions about your surroundings.
- To maintain an interaction history (Q&A) accessible only to you.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
- Images and video frames sent to our servers are processed in real-time and are **not** permanently stored after the analysis is complete, except for specific snapshots explicitly saved to your history log.
- You can request the deletion of your account and all associated interaction history by contacting us at [CONTACT EMAIL PLACEHOLDER].

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
- Google Play Services
- Firebase Authentication
- Picovoice (offline wake word detection, no audio sent to their servers)
