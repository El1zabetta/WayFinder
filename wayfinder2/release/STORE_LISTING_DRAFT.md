# WayFinder Store Listing Texts

## English
**App Name:** WayFinder
**Subtitle / Short Description:** AI-powered navigation assistant for the visually impaired.

**Full Description:**
WayFinder is an advanced AI assistant designed to help blind and visually impaired individuals understand their surroundings. By utilizing your smartphone's camera and microphone, WayFinder acts as a smart, conversational companion that analyzes the environment in real-time.

Features:
- **Egocentric Navigation:** Point your camera forward and WayFinder will describe obstacles and safe paths.
- **Ask Anything:** Use the voice assistant to ask specific questions like "Is there a crosswalk ahead?" or "Where is the nearest chair?".
- **Offline Wake Word:** Simply say "WayFinder" to activate the assistant completely hands-free.
- **Threat Detection:** Get haptic and audio feedback when potential hazards are detected in your path.
- **Accessible Design:** Built from the ground up to work seamlessly with TalkBack/VoiceOver screen readers.

*Safety Warning: WayFinder is an experimental aid and not a replacement for a white cane, guide dog, or human assistance. Always prioritize your primary mobility aids.*
