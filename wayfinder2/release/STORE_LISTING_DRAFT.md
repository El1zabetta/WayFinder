# WayFinder Store Listing Copy

## Tagline
AI-powered assistive companion for the visually impaired.

## Short Description (Google Play, max 80 chars)
AI assistant that describes surroundings and answers questions by voice.

## Full Description (Google Play)

WayFinder is an AI-powered assistive application designed to help blind and visually impaired individuals understand their surroundings. Using your smartphone's camera and microphone, WayFinder provides short, spoken descriptions of the environment to support independent navigation.

### Features
- **Ask by Voice:** Say "WayFinder" to activate, then ask any question about your surroundings. Get a short, spoken answer.
- **Analyze Surroundings:** Tap the Analyze button to receive a scene description with obstacle detection and directional guidance.
- **Obstacle Alerts:** Receive haptic and audio feedback when potential hazards are detected in your path.
- **Repeat & Stop:** Tap to repeat the last response or stop speech at any time.
- **History:** Review past questions and answers in your interaction history.
- **Offline Wake Word:** The "WayFinder" wake word is detected entirely on-device — no audio is sent to external servers for activation.
- **Accessible Design:** Built to work with TalkBack (Android) and VoiceOver (iOS) screen readers.

### Important Safety Information
WayFinder is an **experimental assistive tool** and is **not a replacement** for:
- A white cane or guide dog
- Professional mobility training
- Human assistance or judgment
- Emergency services

AI analysis may be inaccurate, delayed, or incomplete. Always prioritize your primary mobility aids and personal awareness.

### Privacy
- Raw camera frames and audio are processed in real time and are **not stored** on our servers.
- Only derived text responses and metadata are retained in your history.
- You can request deletion of all your data at any time.
